About

I'm a fullstack developer who enjoys owning a product end to end — from designing the database schema to shipping a polished, responsive UI. My comfort zone is the JavaScript ecosystem: Node on the server, either PostgreSQL or MongoDB depending on the data shape, and Tailwind for fast, consistent interfaces.

I care most about systems that hold up under real usage: clean APIs, sensible data models, and frontends that don't fall apart on a phone. Available for freelance and contract engagements.
