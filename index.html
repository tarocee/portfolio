About

I help big or small teams and startups ship fullstack products without the coordination overhead of hiring separate frontend and backend developers.

I work across the JavaScript ecosystem — React and Next.js on the frontend, Node.js on the backend, PostgreSQL or MongoDB depending on the data, and Tailwind for fast, consistent interfaces. One person owning the whole stack means fewer handoffs, faster iteration, and a single point of accountability from schema to deploy.

I care most about systems that hold up under real usage: clean APIs, sensible data models, and frontends that don't fall apart on a phone. Currently open to remote, freelance, and contract roles.
